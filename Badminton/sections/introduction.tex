
\section{Introduction}

This report presents a systematic analysis of badminton
match records across three formats: Singles (1v1),
Doubles (2v2), and Mixed Doubles (Mix).

The recorded scores are organised chronologically and
examined using statistical methods to understand
performance, consistency, and changes in scoring
behaviour.

\subsection{Objectives}

The report has three primary objectives.

\subsubsection{Performance Analysis}

The first objective is to analyse the recorded match
results using descriptive statistics.

This includes calculating averages, standard deviations,
total scores, and net results. These quantities help
describe scoring performance and the degree of
variation across matches.

\subsubsection{Next-Game Prediction}

The second objective is to develop a statistical model
that estimates the expected score in the next game.

A least-squares regression model will be considered
using the historical match records. The objective is
to identify whether the recorded scoring pattern
provides a useful basis for estimating a future result.

The prediction represents a statistical estimate,
not a guaranteed outcome.

\subsubsection{Interpretation}

The final objective is to interpret the calculated
statistics and graphical results.

The analysis will examine differences between match
formats, scoring consistency, trends, and the limitations
of making predictions from a finite set of observations.

\subsection{Match Formats}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]

    \item \textbf{Singles (1v1):}
    One player competes against one opponent.

    \item \textbf{Doubles (2v2):}
    Two players compete against two opponents.

    \item \textbf{Mixed Doubles (Mix):}
    Each team consists of one male and one female player.

\end{itemize}

\subsection{Scoring Convention}

The recorded scores are interpreted as follows:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]

    \item \textbf{Win:} Points scored by our team.
    \item \textbf{Lose:} Points scored by the opponent.

\end{itemize}

The net result is defined as

\[
N = W-L
\]

where \(W\) represents the Win score and \(L\)
represents the Lose score.

A positive net result indicates a higher score
for our team, while a negative value indicates
a higher score for the opponent.

\subsection{Treatment of Missing Entries}

If one score is missing while the other is recorded,
the missing score is considered zero.

If both scores are absent for a particular match
format on a given date, that entry is considered
an absent match and is excluded from the corresponding
table and calculations.
